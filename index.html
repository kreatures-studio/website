Slide 01 / 06 · Who we are

Small team,
full stack
of game skills

Kreatures is a game studio based at the Polo de Contenido Digital in Spain. We build games with other studios and brands: not as an outsourcer that disappears, but as a team that sits inside your pipeline until the project ships.

Every creature in our bestiary is one of the disciplines we cover — code, 2D and 3D art, animation, QA. Pick the ones you need, or hand us the whole slice of work.
